5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Exercise

When emotions feel overwhelming, tap each step as you anchor yourself in the present room.

5
5 Things You Can See
Look around the room and name five distinct objects, colors, or shapes.
4
4 Things You Can Physically Feel
Notice your feet on the floor, the texture of fabric, or the support of your chair.
3
3 Things You Can Hear
Listen for three ambient sounds in the distance or close by.
2
2 Things You Can Smell or Sense in the Air
Take a slow breath through your nose and notice the temperature of the air.
